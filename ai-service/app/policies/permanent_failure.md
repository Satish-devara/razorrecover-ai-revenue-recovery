# Permanent Failure Recovery Policy

Policy ID: POL-PERMANENT-001

## Trigger

Use this policy when the payment failure is permanent or cannot safely be recovered automatically.

## Recommended Action

ESCALATE

## Rules

- Never automatically retry a permanent failure.
- Do not modify the payment amount.
- Escalate the recovery case for review or customer support.
- Record the reason for escalation in the audit trail.

## Reason

Retrying a known permanent failure wastes payment attempts and can create unnecessary customer friction.
